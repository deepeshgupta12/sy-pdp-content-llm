Brigade Citrine is a pioneering residential project from Brigade Group, located at Budigere Cross near Old Madras Road, North Bangalore. Spanning 4.2 acres, this premium development features 420 elegantly designed 1, 2, 3, and 4 BHK apartments across two striking towers of 30 floors each. As India’s first net-zero carbon residential project, Brigade Citrine sets a new benchmark for sustainable, future-ready living, blending modern architecture with eco-conscious innovation.
