Sattva Lumina is a premium residential township by Sattva Group, nestled in 13.88 acres of serene space in Yelahanka, Bangalore. Comprising 8 high-rise blocks with G+29 floors, it offers 1,553 thoughtfully designed studio, 1, 2, and 3 BHK apartments overlooking the tranquil Nagadasanahalli Lake. This expansive township combines spacious Vaastu-compliant homes with over 40 world-class amenities, making it one of the largest and most sought-after residential launches in North Bangalore for 2024.
