Shriram Serenity is a premium residential development by Shriram Properties, located in the fast-evolving neighbourhood of Yelahanka, Bangalore. Spanning 3.83 acres, this thoughtfully planned project offers 270 elegant 2 and 3 BHK apartments across 3 high-rise towers (2B + G + 12 floors), designed to meet the needs of modern urban lifestyles. With unit sizes ranging from 1,065 sq. ft. to 1,755 sq. ft., each apartment boasts smart layouts, ample natural light, and spacious interiors. The meticulously designed master plan ensures a perfect blend of privacy, greenery, and community living, making Shriram Serenity an ideal investment for families, professionals, and discerning homebuyers seeking quality living in North Bangalore.

About Shriram Properties

Shriram Properties is one of South India’s leading residential real estate development companies, primarily focused on the mid-market and mid-premium housing segments, with a growing footprint in plotted developments as well.

Since its inception in Bengaluru in 2000, Shriram Properties successfully expanded operations to other major cities across South India, including Chennai, Coimbatore, and Visakhapatnam, while also establishing a strong presence in Kolkata, East India, with a landmark mixed-use development underway.

As of March 31, 2025, Shriram Properties has delivered 48 projects spanning a total saleable area of 27.6 million sq. ft. their robust development pipeline includes 37 upcoming projects covering 36.3 million sq. ft., underscoring our commitment to building vibrant, high-quality residential communities across key urban centers.
