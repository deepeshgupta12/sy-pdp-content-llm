FAQs

Is this a freehold development?

Land tenure varies by pocket and scheme. Ask for the title chain, land-use certificate, and sale deed draft; have a property lawyer confirm freehold status before token payment.

Are pets allowed inside the community?

Most NCR housing societies allow pets with by-laws on leash use, noise, and common area hygiene. Request the society’s current house rules or Model By-Laws before moving in.

What kind of power backup is provided?

The project lists power backup among core conveniences. Confirm the DG capacity, coverage (common areas vs. inside units), and per-unit backup load during the site visit.

Is municipal water available year-round?

The listing cites 24x7 water supply. In practice, availability often blends municipal and on-site storage/booster systems. Check supply source, storage capacity, and pressure at top floors.

How close is public transit?

Road access is immediate via the bypass. The Delhi–Ghaziabad–Meerut RRTS priority section up to Duhai Depot is operational, improving regional connectivity (NCRTC). Verify last-mile options from the site.

What extra costs should buyers expect beyond the base price?

Budget for stamp duty and registration (per IGRSUP), documentation charges, any PLC/parking, society corpus, monthly CAM, and GST if applicable to your unit’s construction stage (CBIC).

Can I get bank home-loan approval for this property?

Most lenders require clear title, approvals, and (if applicable) valid RERA registration. Share the document set with your preferred bank for a preliminary credit and legal appraisal.
