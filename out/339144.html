Project Overview

Signature Global Cloverdale — Sector 71, Gurgaon

Who Is It For

This is a premium, HRERA-registered residential enclave off the Southern Peripheral Road, planned as large-format apartments with a lifestyle clubhouse and curated greens. It suits end-users and investors seeking generous layouts, robust leisure facilities, and quick east–west connectivity across Golf Course Extension Road, Sohna Road, and NH-48.

Key takeaways: - Large homes: three- and four-bedroom apartments, starting around 2,095 sq ft. - Lifestyle-first planning: heated pool, mini theatre, café, library, and a full-service clubhouse. - Sustainability: IGBC-certified building with rainwater harvesting, STP, and ample landscaping.

Location Connectivity

The site sits in Sector 71, just off the Southern Peripheral Road (SPR), enabling quick hops to Golf Course Extension Road, Sohna Road, and the NH-48 spine toward Delhi–Gurugram–Manesar. This micro-market is prized for commute efficiency to offices, schools, hospitals, and retail hubs.
