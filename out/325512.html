Who Is It For

Krisala Shakai suits urban minimalists and on‑the‑go professionals who want compact, efficient living in Tathawade with quick city access. With 0 and 1 BHK formats (entry carpet about 211 sq ft), it’s ideal for singles, young couples, and frequent travelers who value lock‑and‑leave convenience over large floor plates. The location is a daily commute win—around 0.5 km from both Kalewadi Main Road and the Mumbai–Bangalore Highway—making cross‑PCMC and west‑Pune drives easier.

Shakai’s Japanese‑inspired focus on community and harmony will appeal if you prefer orderly, purpose‑led spaces and an amenity‑rich campus: multi‑brand retail and a café downstairs for errands and quick breaks; a conference room for WFH spillover; and wellness options like a gym, yoga areas, jogging/cycle track, and indoor games. Safety is addressed with 24x7 security and CCTV, while central greens soften the urban edge of Tathawade.

Investors looking at studio/1 BHK assets in a growth corridor may find the price band (about Rs 44.30 L to Rs 1.02 Cr) and mixed‑use convenience attractive for future rental and exit flexibility. Larger families seeking expansive layouts aren’t the core audience; those starting out or downsizing for connectivity, lower maintenance, and everyday convenience are. Registered with MahaRERA (P52100079401) for added compliance assurance.
